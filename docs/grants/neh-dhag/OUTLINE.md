## 1. Overview of the Project — target: 1 page

**Purpose.** Concise, non-specialist statement of the main goal, key activities, audiences, expected outputs, and the tier requested.

**Source paragraphs.** ACADIA paper Section 1 paragraphs 1–3 (`source-material/ACADIA_2026_paper.md` paragraphs immediately after the abstract).

**Claims to foreground.**
- Architecture is a humanities discipline whose pedagogy is structurally ocularcentric.
- The Accessibility Harness is a configuration pattern (not a tool, not a CAD substitute) that lets a BLV student author CAD geometry independently inside the same studio as sighted peers.
- Daniel Bein is co-designer.
- Tier II will refine the pattern, formalize the tactile-graphic standards, evaluate transferability at partner sites, and release the system openly.

**Must add (not in ACADIA paper).**
- Tier statement: "We request a DHAG Tier II award of $X over Y months."
- Named partner institutions (pending answer to IDEAS.md question 2).
- One-sentence statement of public-humanities deliverable (the studio-adoption guide).

## 2. Statement of Significance and Impact — target: 1.5 pages

**Purpose.** Argument for how the project benefits humanities disciplines and addresses a need for specific audiences. This is the section the review criteria weight most heavily.

**Source paragraphs.** ACADIA paper Section 1 paragraph 3 (the parity-redistribution-of-interdependence framing); Section 5 entire (Implications for Adaptive Studio Practice); Section 6 first paragraph (the two contributions).

**Claims to foreground.**
- The four candidate implications for studio practice (explicit textual design state, multi-modal critique, AI-scaffolded verification, communication protocols designed for access) named honestly as candidate hypotheses drawn from a single case — and the Tier II plan to begin testing them at partner sites.
- The "curb-cut" argument at epistemological level: designing for blindness as the primary case reveals technical paradigms (semantic-first state, sense-agnostic representation, auditability) that benefit all students.
- Disability knowledge as expertise — Daniel Bein as crip-technoscientist co-designer whose lived experience generates architectural knowledge inaccessible to sighted tool designers.
- Methodological substrate-agnosticism — Claude is the current LLM but the pattern survives model and vendor changes.

**Must add.**
- Audiences named concretely: BLV architecture students nationally; architecture faculty considering accessibility; DH researchers building accessible interfaces to other ocularcentric disciplines (art history, archaeology, museum studies); tactile-graphics standards community.
- Field-of-impact statement explicit about humanities scope, not just architecture.

## 3. Environmental Scan — target: 1.5 pages

**Purpose.** Survey of related work and how this project differs. Maps onto NEH's "what else is happening in this space" expectation.

**Source paragraphs.** ACADIA paper Section 3 entire — *Two Gaps in the Literature*. Subsections 3.1 (Accessible CAD and Tactile Architectural Communication), 3.2 (Disability Studies and Architectural Ocularcentrism), 3.3 (LLM-Driven CAD and Agentic Harnesses), 3.4 (The Two Gaps).

**Claims to foreground.**
- The two gaps: (a) accessible-CAD work has been tool-oriented rather than pattern-oriented; (b) LLM agent-harness pattern has not been adapted for accessibility.
- Direct positioning relative to A11yShape (Zhang et al. 2025), TACTICS (Way & Barner 1997), RainbowTact (Ka & Kim 2024), Text2CAD / CAD-Llama / CAD2Program, rhino-mcp.
- Disability studies grounding: Hamraie 2017, Boys 2014, DisOrdinary Architecture Project, Schön 1987.

**Must add.**
- Survey of prior NEH ODH funded projects in the accessibility space — at least one cited as field context (lookup task during drafting).
- DH-venue work (e.g., relevant ADHO-conference projects) that the ACADIA paper does not cite because ACADIA is an architecture venue.

## 7. Data Management Plan — target: separate 2-page attachment, skeleton only here

**Purpose.** Per NEH ODH requirements (max 2 pages). Drafted later; skeleton here for tracking.

**Sections to cover when drafted.**

- Formats and standards: source code as Git repositories; state files as documented JSON schemas; transcripts as plain text with linked audio; photographs and video in archival formats (TIFF, lossless audio, FFV1 video).

- Long-term preservation: institutional repository at UIUC; community-maintained GitHub for code; commitment to a specified preservation horizon post-grant.
