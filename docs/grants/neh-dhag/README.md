# NEH DHAG Proposal — Radical Accessibility Project

**Target program:** NEH Office of Digital Humanities, Digital Humanities Advancement Grants (DHAG), **Tier II ($75,000–$150,000)**.

**Status:** Brainstorm + outline only. Narrative prose, budget numbers, Data Management Plan, bio sketches, and letters of commitment are not yet drafted — they will be added in later rounds on this branch.

**Why DHAG, in one sentence.** The Radical Accessibility Project advances the digital humanities by making architectural design — a humanities-adjacent discipline whose pedagogy and culture are deeply ocularcentric — authorable by blind and low-vision students through an LLM-mediated configuration pattern (the Accessibility Harness) that decouples design state from any single sensory channel.

## Files in this directory

- **`PROGRAM_NOTES.md`** — distilled DHAG Tier II requirements, sourced from the NEH NOFO and DHAG landing page. Treat as the spec sheet every narrative section must satisfy.
- **`SOURCES.md`** — index of internal source material (ACADIA 2026 paper, prior ACADIA drafts, MANUAL, MCP_GUIDE, README, Anthropic-pitch raw URLs) with notes on what carries over and what must be reframed.
- **`IDEAS.md`** — brainstorm: why DHAG fits, what shifts from the ACADIA voice to the NEH voice, Tier II scope hypothesis, known risks, open questions for the team.
- **`OUTLINE.md`** — section-by-section outline of the NEH narrative, mapped 1:1 onto DHAG's required sections, with page-budget targets and source-paragraph pointers into the ACADIA paper.
- **`source-material/ACADIA_2026_paper.md`** — full text of the ACADIA 2026 paper extracted from the user-uploaded docx. Canonical source for quoting and citing during drafting.
